Hide a player

A hidden player drops off every leaderboard at once. They can still sign in and play, and nothing is deleted — Unhide puts them straight back. Use it for a rude name, a name pretending to be staff, or results nobody could really get. Flagged results (a daily solve under 10 seconds, a sprint over 20 puzzles) are already kept off the boards on their own.

Make a theme from a spreadsheet

Two columns: the word, then a one-line fact players see when they find it. A header row is optional. Words are 3–9 letters, A–Z only. A theme needs 5 to 50 usable words; 12 or more gives players variety.
